A workspace count walks one record at a time. The atom listing writes one atom at a time. Both give the freed pages back.
A pack of 3000 live atoms with 384-d vectors used 260036 KiB of resident memory after a search, a full listing and a workspace count. After the change it used 111476 KiB.
The listing peak fell from 316484 KiB to 135324 KiB.
Supervised packsetd starts the writer again when it is killed or it exits with an error. A stop signal ends the loop.
On a refused connection, or a 503 or 429, the client retries until its request budget runs out. It starts the local writer once when PACKSET_HOME is set. The error is one sentence.
