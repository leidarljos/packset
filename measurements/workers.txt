Intel Xeon (family 6, model 207), 8 cores, glibc 2.39, Linux 6.12.94+
Default thread stack 8192 KiB. Each thread mallocs 64 KiB and touches it.

VmSize grows by one 8 MiB stack plus one 64 MiB arena a thread.
The 64 MiB is glibc HEAP_MAX_SIZE on 64-bit: twice DEFAULT_MMAP_THRESHOLD_MAX, which is 4 MiB times sizeof(long) (glibc 2.39 malloc/malloc.c and malloc/arena.c).
VmRSS does not grow by that. The reservation is address space, and it is the most an arena can become once the thread allocates into it.
